Prayagraj Magh Mela 2027

Plan Your Sacred Stay at Triveni Sangam

Every winter, Prayagraj becomes a spiritual destination as pilgrims gather at the sacred Triveni Sangam — the confluence of the Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical Saraswati. Magh Mela brings together faith, devotion, Kalpavas, holy bathing and India's rich spiritual traditions.

Saints, sadhus and spiritual seekers arrive from across the country to participate in devotional activities, cultural experiences and sacred observances along the ghats.

Important Snan Dates

Key bathing and observance days during Prayagraj Magh Mela 2027.

14 Jan 2027 Makar Sankranti An auspicious day for holy bathing and spiritual observance.
22 Jan 2027 Paush Purnima Traditionally associated with the beginning of Kalpavas.
6 Feb 2027 Mauni Amavasya One of the most spiritually significant bathing days.
11 Feb 2027 Basant Panchami A festival associated with Saraswati, wisdom and the arrival of spring.
20 Feb 2027 Maghi Purnima An important full-moon bathing day and period of spiritual observance.
6 Mar 2027 Maha Shivratri A sacred occasion dedicated to Lord Shiva and an important concluding observance.
